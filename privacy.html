Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

About the application

Hermes YouTube Desktop is a personal desktop application used to upload videos to YouTube through the YouTube Data API.

Google account access

The application requests Google OAuth authorization to upload videos to the authorized YouTube channel.

Data storage

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. They are not published on this website.

Data sharing

The application communicates with Google APIs to perform authorized YouTube operations. It does not sell personal information.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked through Google Account permissions .

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application developer using the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
